# Playbook: FDE: first customer meeting with FDE


This playbook is for the FDE running FDE Orchestration Layer engagements. The skill files describe what an agent does. This playbook describes what *you* do.

## Before the session

1. **Confirm scope.** One unit of work in scope. Push back on scope expansion.
2. **Confirm attendees.** Workload owner + the right compliance / engineering / product voice. Reschedule if the right people are not in the room.
3. **Confirm time.** 90 minutes minimum. Anything shorter rushes the discovery and degrades the artefact.
4. **Pre-read.** Send the customer the program steering files 24 hours ahead.

## In the session

1. **Open with the AIM context.** Why this program is relevant given the customer's AIM scores. Five minutes.
2. **Walk the procedure.** Use the skill files as your checklist. Do not skip steps to save time; the artefact's value is the conversation.
3. **Capture findings live.** Every gap becomes a finding. Every finding gets an owner and a deadline before the session ends.
4. **Close with sign-off plan.** Who reviews the draft, when, and how the customer signs off.

## After the session

1. Within 24 hours: send the draft artefact.
2. Within 1 week: signed-off final.
3. Within 30 days: re-check progress on every high-severity finding.

## Red flags

- The right voice is not in the room (compliance / engineering / product depending on program). Reschedule.
- "We'll figure that out later" answers from the customer. Document as a finding.
- The conversation drifts to other programs. Note them, schedule separately, return to this one.
