# Citizen-Facing AI Transparency

## Rules

### 1. Disclosure Obligations
- Inform citizens when AI is used in processing their case/application
- Provide the basis for AI-assisted decisions in language citizens understand
- Publish AI system registry (which systems are in use, for what purpose)

### 2. Explainability Standards
- Every AI output used in citizen-facing decisions must have an explanation
- Explanation must cite: input factors, model reasoning, confidence level
- "The AI said so" is never an acceptable explanation to a citizen

### 3. Right to Human Review
- Citizens can request human review of any AI-influenced decision
- Human reviewers must have authority to override AI recommendations
- Review request must be processed within published SLA (typically 10 business days)

### 4. Bias Monitoring
- Regular demographic fairness audits (quarterly minimum)
- Report differential outcomes by protected characteristics
- Immediate remediation if disparate impact detected
- Public reporting of fairness metrics (annual minimum)

### 5. Algorithmic Impact Assessment
- Before deploying citizen-facing AI: conduct impact assessment
- Assess: privacy, fairness, transparency, accountability, safety
- Publish summary of assessment (redacting security-sensitive details)
- Re-assess on significant model updates
