# Model Risk Governance

Financial-services customers typically operate under a model risk management framework that owns the lifecycle of quantitative models. When AI systems touch decisions a risk function already oversees, align FDE artifacts to that framework rather than creating a parallel process.

## Hand-off to model risk

- Identify the customer's model risk owner and confirm their intake format.
- Map FDE exit criteria to the customer's validation evidence requirements.
- Record independent review outcomes in the customer's system of record.

## Escalation

Any AI system whose outputs influence capital, credit, pricing, or supervisory reporting should follow the customer's highest-tier model governance path, including independent validation prior to production use.
